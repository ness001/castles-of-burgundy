TABLETOP FOLEY ADDITIONS - 2026-09-30

These ten cues use licensed source samples. Crate loading, a gate, a mine hit,
and a stamp are foley interpretations of the physical sounds listed below,
not claims that a specific historical prop was field-recorded. No synthesized
oscillators or generated audio were added.

Common modifications: mono downmix; 44,100 Hz / 64 kbit/s MP3; source metadata
removed; excerpting/layering as stated per file; measured gain toward -25 dBFS
RMS, followed by a -8 dBFS peak limiter (1 ms attack, 35 ms release, no makeup
gain), and short fades. MP3 encoding may slightly change peak values.
Durations refer to decoded audio. Source pitch was not changed.
